Built with love, luxury and your comfort in mind

Red Deer Nail Lounge opened its new space on Gasoline Alley in 2026. White marble, crystal chandeliers, gold-framed walls and massage chairs at every pedicure station.

The people

May, Kevin and the team

The lounge is owned by May and her husband Kevin. Some of May's clients have followed her for more than 17 years, and many say the same thing: you come for the nails and come back for the people.

  • May is known for detailed, hand-painted nail art and sets that last.
  • Kevin and Tony are behind the pedicures that clients call the best they have had.
  • The whole team greets regulars by name and keeps the salon spotless.
